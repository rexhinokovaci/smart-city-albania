If a camera has filmed you, the footage in which you appear is **personal data**. Law No. 124/2024 "On the protection of personal data" is aligned with the EU GDPR. It gives you the right to know what data is held about you and to obtain a copy.

> This guide is informational and is not legal advice. For specific cases, consult a lawyer or the Commissioner.

The Albanian version is [Si të kërkosh pamjet e kamerës ku shfaqesh ti](../si-te-kerkosh-pamjet-e-kameres/).

## 1. Find out who operates the camera

- **Smart City and police cameras**: the controller is the State Police / Ministry of Interior.
- **Municipal cameras**: the controller is the relevant municipality.
- **Private cameras** (shops, banks, apartment buildings): the controller is the private entity, which is expected to display an information sign.

On the [map](../../) check the "Operator" field when it is known.

## 2. Send a written request

Write an email or formal letter that includes:

1. Your name and a way to identify you.
2. **The date, time (as precisely as you can) and place** where you passed the camera.
3. A description that helps them find you in the footage (clothing, vehicle).
4. The request itself, for example: "Under the right of access in Law No. 124/2024, I request a copy of the personal data concerning me, together with information on the purpose of processing, the retention period and the recipients of the data."

**Act quickly.** Many systems automatically delete footage after a few days. Retention periods are one of the questions that remain publicly unanswered for the Smart City programme; see [EU standards on surveillance](../standardet-e-be-per-mbikeqyrjen/).

## 3. Response deadline

The controller should respond **within 30 days**. The law allows limited extensions. Access can be restricted in some cases, for example to protect an investigation, but a refusal should be reasoned.

## 4. If there is no answer: complain to the Commissioner

The Commissioner for the Right to Information and Protection of Personal Data (IDP) is the independent supervisory authority. You can file a complaint through the official site [idp.al](https://idp.al). Attach a copy of your request and any reply you received. See also [how to complain to the IDP](../si-t-i-ankohesh-komisionerit-idp-per-kamerat/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can someone else request my footage?** No. Footage of other people is also protected and is usually blurred before it is handed over.

**Do I have to explain why I want it?** No. The right of access does not require a justification.

**What about traffic cameras and fines?** If you receive a fine from a camera, you have the right to see the photographic evidence supporting it.

**What about licence plate readers?** See [what ANPR cameras are](../kamerat-qe-lexojne-targat-alpr/) and [how to file a freedom-of-information request](../si-te-besh-kerkese-per-informim-per-kamerat/).
